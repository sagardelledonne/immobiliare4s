Company Profile

Thermal management for power electronics.

Who we are

Bosari Thermal Management S.r.l. (BTM) is based in Opera, on the edge of Milan, and deals with thermal management for power electronics.

Its product lines are the ATLANTIS water coolers, the COLUMBUS heat exchangers for electronic cabinets and the U-COOL heat pipe systems.

Our story

Bosari Thermal Management was founded in Opera in 1996, with the idea of bringing more efficient, lighter and more reliable cooling to power electronics. Over time the range has grown into three lines: the ATLANTIS water coolers, the COLUMBUS exchangers for electronic cabinets and the U-COOL heat pipe systems.

We work closely with our customers' designers: many solutions, such as the U-COOL, start from their specification.

Our approach

Tailor-made

Solutions adapt to the customer: cooler contact diameter up to 55 or 85 mm, aluminium or titanium fluid nipples, control systems on request. The U-COOL system was born from a customer's specification.

Controlled materials

Materials and components are selected and quality controlled to ensure compliance with the requested specifications.

Made for severe environments

Products for industrial and railway applications, with shock and vibration tests as per IEC 77 §16.

How we work

  1. ListeningWe collect the specification: power to dissipate, temperatures, space and standards.
  2. DesignWe size the thermal solution and prepare the drawing.
  3. PrototypeWe build a sample to verify the solution.
  4. ProductionWe produce in series with selected and controlled materials.
  5. SupportWe follow the customer in choice, installation and use.

Sectors

  • Heavy industry and welding
  • Electronic cabinets and environmental chambers
