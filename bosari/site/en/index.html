Dissipating a lot of heat in a small space

Bosari Thermal Management (BTM) designs and manufactures solutions to remove the heat of power electronic components such as thyristors, GTOs and IGBTs, and to cool electronic cabinets and environmental chambers.

Product lines

ATLANTIS® · Water coolers

Aluminium water coolers for thyristors, GTOs and IGBTs: up to 4,000 W dissipated on both sides.

Discover the line →

COLUMBUS · Heat exchangers for electronic cabinets

Counterflow exchangers with separated circuits and no air exchange between cabinet and ambient, compliant with IP55 and NEMA 12.

Discover the line →

U-COOL® · Heat pipe cooling systems

Natural-convection heat pipe coolers, designed for power electronics on railway vehicles.
