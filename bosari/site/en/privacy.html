Privacy notice

How we handle personal data collected through this website.

Data controller

Bosari Thermal Management S.r.l., Via Eugenio Montale, 14, 20090 Opera (MI), Italy. Email: info@bosari.it.

What data we collect

Only what you write to us: name, company, email and message text, when you prepare a request from the contact form. The form does not send anything by itself: it opens your email program, and the data reaches us only if you send the message.

Why and on what basis

To answer your request for information or a quotation (pre-contractual measures taken at your request, art. 6.1.b GDPR). The data is not used for other purposes nor passed to third parties.

For how long

For the time needed to answer and, if a business relationship starts, for the duration of legal obligations.

Cookies and third-party services

The site uses no profiling cookies and no analytics tools. The map on the Contacts page is a Google Maps service and is loaded only after your explicit consent, with the “Load the map” button.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection (arts. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to info@bosari.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority.
