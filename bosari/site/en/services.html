Services

From specification to product, and beyond.

Besides the catalogue products, we help customers solve their thermal problem, from the first analysis to support.

Thermal analysis and sizing

We start from the power to dissipate, the allowed temperatures and the available space to propose the most suitable solution.

Design and prototypes

Drawing, choice of materials and a sample to verify the solution before series production.

Tests and verifications

Thermal and mechanical checks referring to the standards required by the customer, for example IEC 77 §16 for shocks and vibrations.

Series production

Coolers and exchangers made with selected and quality-controlled materials and components.

Technical support

Help with the choice, installation and use of Bosari products.
